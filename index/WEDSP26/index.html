Emma and Johannes' Wedding

Emma and Johannes' wedding was a special one. A highlight was the Lutheran church where the ceremony took place that had such beautiful natural light and wood tones. I spent an hour or so with the couple before the ceremony to take some couples photos, which was one of my favorite moments of the day. The weather that day started out cloudy and overcast but by the time the reception started, the sun had come out. Scroll to the bottom for film.
