Photos from Seaalpsee

Seaalpsee and Ebenalp are part of a hike that my friends at Bodenseehof take the new group of students on every year. I've been able to go on this hike a handful of times and it's always been a highlight. Knowing this time I would be doing the hike, I brought my digital camera with me and decided to do a few experiments with shutter drag, as well as just capturing moments through the day.
