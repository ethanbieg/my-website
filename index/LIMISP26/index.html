Liminalität at HFU

Liminalität was a unique art exhibition put on by my University, HFU, that I helped organize with a few others. It was an exploration in ideas about liminality and being in the middle of past and future. Many of the design and art students from our university created interactive exhibits, a few of which I've captured here.
