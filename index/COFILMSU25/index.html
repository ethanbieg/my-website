Moments on color film in the summer

Summer 2025 contained many incredibly rich and sacred moments for me and who I've been becoming. Over 3 separate rolls of film I captured moments in Europe and in America, feeling drawn specifically to the wildness of Summer in its beauty and expression. I found this in images of friendships, landscapes, adventures, and love. I remember distinctly the day I received my film scans back and marveled at the beauty of what God did in that time.
