Black & White Winter Film

I've recently been falling in love with the B/W look. I shot this roll on my Nikkormat film camera, and was pleasantly surprised with how many photos I loved from the set. I felt this roll in particular captured part of the feeling of the intensity of winter in Europe, the stark contrast between darkness and light, the harsh cold, and the warmth of Christmas.
